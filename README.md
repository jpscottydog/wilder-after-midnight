## 🎬 About This Project

**"Wilder After Midnight: The Ballad of June Wilder"** is an upcoming music video from The AI Sessions—a creative laboratory where human vision meets machine intelligence to craft cinematic experiences that blur the line between digital creation and raw human emotion.

This page serves as the official character guide and information hub for the project, introducing audiences to the women of the El Camino universe before the video drops.

## 🌵 The Story

Somewhere between Last Chance and No Tomorrow, three women made a pact. They'd ride together, fight together, and maybe—just maybe—find something worth stopping for.

"Wilder After Midnight" isn't just a music video. It's a visual poem about escape and belonging, about the families we choose and the ghosts we can't shake. Set to an original blues instrumental that builds from a whisper to a roar, the story unfolds across desert highways, neon-lit diners, and the spaces between streetlights where secrets live.

**No dialogue. No exposition. Just the wail of electric guitars, the hum of V8 engines, and the silent understanding between women who've learned that trust is the only currency that matters.**

## 👥 The Characters

### June Wilder (The Protagonist)
Behind the wheel of her '69 Camaro, she's equal parts fugitive and seeker. The highway is her confessional, the night sky her only witness.

### Lena Cross (The Strategist)
The architect of the trio's survival. Cold, calculating, and always three moves ahead. Her '65 Mustang is as pristine as her reputation is complicated.

### Scarlett Vance (The Wild Card)
If Lena is the brain and June is the heart, Scarlett is the gasoline. She drives a '57 Bel Air the color of dried blood and has a laugh that could charm the devil.

### Sheriff Harlan (The Law)
Twenty years chasing ghosts up and down Highway 95. Not sure what he's looking for anymore—redemption, closure, or just a reason to keep the badge polished.

## 🚗 The Machines

- **1969 Camaro SS** - June's ride. Midnight blue with a 396 under the hood.
- **1965 Mustang GT** - Lena's precision instrument. Raven black, immaculate.
- **1957 Bel Air** - Scarlett's statement piece. Candy apple red with more chrome than sense.
- **1972 Dodge Polara** - Sheriff Harlan's cruiser. White with a gold star.
